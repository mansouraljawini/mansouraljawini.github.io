Executive communications

Reading a 137-page consultation package for the decisions inside it

International Monetary Fund  ·  Intern, Office of the Executive Director for Saudi Arabia  ·  May – August 2024

Challenge

Article IV consultation packages run to well over a hundred pages of macroeconomic, fiscal and structural analysis. Executive Directors need the decision-relevant content, not the document.

Approach

Read the public package end to end, prioritized risks and outlook over description, and rebuilt the material as a visual brief — summary first, evidence second, qualitative judgment carried through.

Output

Executive-ready country briefs across five economies — China, Switzerland, Zambia, Equatorial Guinea and Somalia. The longest source package was reduced from 137 pages to eight, structured for a reader with minutes rather than hours. Zambia ran 142 pages to seventeen; Switzerland, 67 to eleven.

Also during the internship

Delivered three executive briefings on debt sustainability and risk frameworks directly to the Executive Director. Managed vendor contracts, venue logistics and stakeholder coordination for an executive event hosting more than ninety IMF and World Bank Group directors.

Substantive briefing example

Zambia — debt restructuring and IMF support. Synthesized the G20 Common Framework, negotiations with bilateral and commercial creditors, debt-sustainability considerations, repayment terms and IMF support through the Extended Credit Facility into a single executive briefing.

All source material referenced is publicly released IMF country documentation.
